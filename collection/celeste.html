Empire · Silk chiffon

Celeste

Celeste sits at the waist, not the hip, with a soft cowl at the back that we drape by hand on each bride during the first fitting — no two are pinned quite the same way. The silk chiffon is dyed in small batches to an ivory that reads warmer than bridal-standard white, closer to the color of a candle than a bedsheet. This is our lightest gown by a wide margin, and the one most often chosen for outdoor ceremonies in July and August.
